## ESTRUTURA DO PROMPT PARA GERAR A ARTE NO GPT IMAGE 2

Para cada slide, você deve gerar o prompt de imagem em inglês seguindo uma destas 4 estruturas de estilo:

### 🌸 Opção A: Presets de Metáfora Orgânica (Objeto Minimalista)
> **Prompt Base:** `High quality macro photograph of a [objeto orgânico, ex: translucent onion slice petals arranged like a blooming lotus flower], centered composition, clean studio lighting, warm soft shadows, placed on a fine off-white cream linen canvas texture background. Minimalist aesthetic, soft dusty rose palette, elegant editorial design, 8k --ar 4:5`

### 🔮 Opção B: Presets de Aura Gradiente & Energia
> **Prompt Base:** `Ethereal grainy aura gradient art, soft blurry translucent human silhouette figure, glowing radiant warm light inside chest center, pastel pink, lavender, and sage green colors, soft ethereal haze, grainy texture, dreamlike spiritual aesthetic, high resolution --ar 4:5`

### 🎨 Opção C: Presets de Pincelada Orgânica / Aquarela
> **Prompt Base:** `Minimalist pink and mauve concentric watercolor spiral paint stroke, raw textured linen paper background, fine handmade paper grain, artistic organic circle, soft blush tones, high detail --ar 4:5`

### 📜 Opção D: Presets Editorial Monocrático (Papel Ameixa / Dusty Rose)
> **Prompt Base:** `Solid dusty rose plum paper texture background, clean minimal layout, subtle handmade paper grain, soft lighting, elegant editorial composition --ar 4:5`
